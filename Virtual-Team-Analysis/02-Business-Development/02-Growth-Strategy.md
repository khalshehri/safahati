## Document Owner: Business Development
## Version: 1.0 | April 2026

## Overview

Safahati's growth strategy is built on three mutually reinforcing engines:

1. **Product-led growth (PLG):** The product itself drives signups and conversion through a frictionless trial experience
2. **Community-led growth:** Arabic-speaking SME and freelancer communities spread the product through word-of-mouth and professional networks
3. **Partnership-led growth:** Agency resellers, payment providers, and government programs create channels that Safahati could not afford to build independently

This document covers the go-to-market strategy, vertical-by-vertical acquisition plans, channel strategy, and the referral/agency reseller model.

---

## Go-to-Market Strategy

### Phase 1: Beachhead (Q2 2026 — First 50 Customers)

**Strategy:** Controlled beta launch targeting high-intent, network-connected individuals who will become advocates. Do not spend on paid ads yet.

**Target:** Saudi freelancers and small professional services businesses in Riyadh with existing digital presence (active on Twitter/X, LinkedIn, or Instagram).

**Tactics:**
- Founder-led outreach: personal messages to 200 target users from the founding team's network
- Twitter/X posts in Arabic demonstrating the product (screen recordings, before/after comparisons)
- Direct outreach to Saudi freelancer WhatsApp groups and Telegram channels
- Offer first 50 beta users 3 months free in exchange for honest feedback
- One-on-one onboarding calls with first 20 users to identify friction points

**Success criteria:** 50 active beta users; 30 published sites; qualitative feedback collected from all

---

### Phase 2: Public Launch (June–August 2026)

**Strategy:** Public launch with targeted paid acquisition in Arabic, combined with content marketing and agency channel activation.

**Target:** Saudi SMEs across Company, Freelancer, Clinic, and Restaurant verticals

**Paid acquisition (SAR 15,000/month budget):**
- Google Search Ads in Arabic: target high-intent keywords ("إنشاء موقع إلكتروني" / "موقع لعيادة")
- Meta (Instagram/Facebook) Ads: visual demos targeting Saudi business owners by industry
- Twitter/X Ads: promoted tweets targeting Saudi freelancer and SME audiences
- Snapchat Ads: Saudi-specific; strong penetration among 25–35 age group

**Content marketing:**
- Arabic blog: 2 posts/week on topics like "كيف تحصل على موقع إلكتروني لعيادتك في السعودية"
- YouTube Arabic: product walkthrough videos, customer success stories, comparison vs. Wix
- Arabic SEO optimization: target informational queries around "موقع إلكتروني" + [industry] + "السعودية"

**Agency channel activation:**
- Direct outreach to 50 Saudi digital agencies (5–50 employees)
- Offer 60-day white-label trial for agencies
- Agency webinar: "كيف تزيد ربحية وكالتك مع صفحاتي" (live demo + Q&A)

---

### Phase 3: Scale (September 2026+)

**Strategy:** Activate government program partnerships, formalize referral program, expand to UAE and Egypt.

**Tactics:**
- Monsha'at partnership: list Safahati as a recommended digital services provider
- HRDF partnership: promote Safahati to freelancers in the Freelance Work Program
- Referral program launch (see below)
- PR: Arabic tech media (Wamda, Argaam, ArabNet) — product launch story
- Events: GITEX Saudi 2026, Saudi Digital Summit, Cityscape Riyadh
- UAE expansion: duplicate Saudi acquisition playbook for UAE market
- Egypt expansion: adapt pricing (EGP-denominated), focus on Cairo-based SMEs

## Agency Reseller Model

### Program Structure

The Safahati Agency Program creates a win-win reseller relationship:

| Tier | Monthly Fee (SAR) | Active Sites Allowed | White-Label | Priority Support | Agency Commission |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Agency Starter | 699 | 10 | Yes | Email | N/A (flat fee model) |
| Agency Pro | 1,299 | 25 | Yes | Chat + Email | N/A |
| Agency Enterprise | 2,499 | Unlimited | Yes | Dedicated contact | Revenue share option |

### How It Works

1. Agency subscribes to an Agency plan on Safahati
2. Agency creates client sub-accounts from their agency dashboard
3. Agency sets their own pricing for clients (Safahati does not dictate client-facing pricing)
4. Agency dashboard shows all client sites, their status, and renewal dates
5. All sites served under the agency's white-label brand (no "Powered by Safahati" unless agency wants it)
6. Agency receives one consolidated monthly invoice from Safahati

### Agency Economics Example

An agency with 10 active client sites, billing each client SAR 400/month:
- Agency income from clients: 10 × SAR 400 = **SAR 4,000/month**
- Agency cost (Safahati Agency Starter): **SAR 699/month**
- Gross margin: **SAR 3,301/month (82.5%)**

This is substantially more profitable than maintaining a custom development capacity for small client websites, which typically yields 30–40% margins.

### Agency Onboarding Process

1. Introductory call + demo (45 minutes)
2. 30-day free Agency Starter trial (waive trial for agencies with ≥ 5 committed client sites)
3. Guided migration of first client site during onboarding (Safahati team support)
4. Agency training session: admin dashboard + client management + customization options
5. Agency certification: "Safahati Certified Agency" badge for their website/marketing

## Referral Program

### Program Design

**Mechanic:** Every active paying subscriber gets a unique referral link. When a referred user becomes a paying subscriber, both parties earn a reward.

| Action | Referrer Reward | Referred User Reward |
|---|---|---|
| Referred user signs up (free trial) | Nothing | 45-day trial (vs. 14 days) |
| Referred user converts to paid plan | 1 free month (credited) | 20% discount on first 3 months |

**Double-sided incentive rationale:** The referrer gets real monetary value (free month = SAR 79–179 value). The new user gets extended trial time, which reduces commitment anxiety and increases trial-to-paid conversion.

### Referral Program Operations

- Tracking: custom referral code appended to signup URL (`safahati.com/register?ref=CODE`)
- Attribution: server-side cookie (30-day window); DB records referral relationship at signup
- Fraud prevention: referred user must provide a valid credit card before reward is credited; free months issued after first payment clears
- Dashboard widget: shows referral count, pending rewards, earned free months
- Email sequence: referral invite template (pre-written in Arabic + English), success notification

### Referral Program Projections

Assuming 10% of active customers make at least one referral, and 30% of referred signups convert:

| Month | Active Customers | Expected Referrals | Converted Referrals | Referral-Driven MRR (SAR) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| June 2026 | 100 | 10 | 3 | 450 |
| August 2026 | 200 | 20 | 6 | 900 |
| October 2026 | 400 | 40 | 12 | 1,800 |
| December 2026 | 800 | 80 | 24 | 3,600 |

By end of 2026, referral program contributes ~15–20% of new customer acquisition at near-zero CAC.

## Growth Metrics Framework

| Metric | Measurement | Target (Q3 2026) |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly signups | New registrations per month | 200 |
| Trial-to-paid conversion | % of trials who pay within 14 days | 25% |
| Day-7 activation rate | % who publish a site within 7 days | 50% |
| Monthly churn rate | % of paying customers who cancel | < 6% |
| CAC (blended) | Total acquisition spend / new paying customers | < SAR 300 |
| LTV (24-month) | ARPU × gross margin × avg customer lifetime | > SAR 3,000 |
| LTV:CAC ratio | LTV divided by CAC | > 10:1 |
| Referral % of new customers | Referral-driven signups / total signups | > 20% |
| Agency plan % of MRR | Agency plan revenue / total MRR | > 25% |
