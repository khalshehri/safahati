# Business Development — Skills & Capabilities Profile
## Role: Business Development Lead, Safahati Platform

---

## Overview

The BD role on Safahati is not a generic SaaS sales function. It requires deep fluency in the Saudi/MENA SME landscape, relationships with local business communities, and the ability to translate a technical multi-tenant platform into a compelling value story for a restaurant owner in Jeddah or a freelance designer in Riyadh. The BD lead must be equally effective in Arabic business culture contexts and in partnership negotiations with payment processors and technology providers.

---

## Core Competency Areas

### 1. MENA Market Intelligence

| Skill | Safahati-Specific Application |
|---|---|
| Saudi SME ecosystem mapping | Identify which ministries, chambers, and programs support the 1.3M+ SMEs in Saudi Arabia |
| Vision 2030 program fluency | Leverage HRDF freelance program, Monsha'at SME support, and NTP digital initiatives as acquisition channels |
| Industry vertical knowledge | Understand specific needs of clinics, law firms, real estate offices — Safahati's top verticals |
| Local payment behavior | Know that Mada penetration in Saudi Arabia exceeds 95% and that many SMEs lack international credit cards |
| Competitor landscape tracking | Monitor Zid, Salla, and local agency pricing quarterly |
| Seasonal business patterns | Ramadan campaign planning, Eid promotions, National Day (September 23) traffic spikes |

### 2. Arabic Business Development

- Conducts sales conversations and partner negotiations in Arabic (Gulf dialect preferred)
- Understands trust-building norms in Saudi B2B: relationships precede transactions; cold outreach is less effective than warm introductions
- Familiar with Saudi corporate governance: most SMEs operate under a sole proprietorship (Establishments license / Majmou'a) or LLC structure
- Understands wasta dynamics: referral-based networks and professional endorsements are highly influential
- Proficient in preparing business proposals and pitch decks in both Arabic and English, with culturally appropriate framing

### 3. SaaS Go-to-Market Execution

- **Product-led growth:** Drives free trial signups, activation, and conversion
- **Outbound sales:** Manages targeted outreach campaigns to SME segments (LinkedIn, Twitter/X, WhatsApp Business)
- **Channel sales:** Structures and manages agency reseller relationships
- **Partnership management:** Negotiates and executes co-marketing and integration agreements
- **Event-led growth:** Represents Safahati at Saudi Cityscape, GITEX Saudi, Monsha'at events

### 4. Partnership Development

The BD lead must be capable of identifying, qualifying, and executing partnerships across several categories:

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| Payment processors | Moyasar, HyperPay, Tabby, Tamara |
| Government digital programs | Monsha'at (SME Authority), HRDF, SDAIA |
| Professional associations | Saudi Bar Association, Saudi Medical Association, RICS Saudi |
| Business accelerators | SVC (Saudi Venture Capital), Flat6Labs Riyadh, Hub71 Abu Dhabi |
| Technology partners | Hetzner, Cloudflare, Resend, Vercel |
| Content / media | Arab News digital, Gig Arabia, Freelance Arabia |

### 5. Pricing & Commercial Modeling

- Sets SAR-denominated pricing based on local benchmarks and willingness-to-pay research
- Models customer acquisition cost (CAC) and lifetime value (LTV) per segment
- Structures agency/reseller pricing with appropriate margin for partners
- Negotiates volume discounts and enterprise agreements
- Understands ZATCA VAT requirements: 15% VAT must be included in all Saudi B2B invoices

### 6. Content-Led Demand Generation

- Writes Arabic-language case studies, success stories, and industry guides
- Manages local social media presence (Twitter/X is dominant in Saudi; Instagram for visual industries)
- Produces comparison content ("Safahati vs. Wix" in Arabic) targeting search intent
- Creates vertical-specific lead magnets: "Complete Guide to Clinic Websites in Saudi Arabia"

### 7. Regulatory & Compliance Awareness

| Area | Requirement |
|---|---|
| ZATCA e-invoicing | All paid invoices require ZATCA-compliant XML/UBL format (Phase 2 active since 2023) |
| Saudi PDPL | Personal data of Saudi residents must meet PDPL standards; data residency awareness required |
| CITC regulations | Communications and IT regulations for platform services |
| Commercial registration | Must have a Saudi commercial registration (CR) or partner with a local entity for certain payment gateway approvals |

### 8. Community Building

- Identifies and cultivates Safahati power users who become advocates
- Builds a network of "Safahati Certified" agencies and freelancers
- Runs community events (virtual and in-person) in Riyadh, Jeddah, and Khobar
- Manages a WhatsApp community for platform customers (dominant professional communication channel in Saudi Arabia)

## Anti-Patterns to Avoid

1. **Targeting enterprise before SME is validated** — the Saudi enterprise segment has long procurement cycles and requires a Saudi CR; start with SME and agency
2. **English-only marketing** — 70%+ of the target audience prefers Arabic for business communication; every piece of BD material must exist in Arabic first
3. **Underestimating relationship length** — Saudi B2B relationships take 2–3 touchpoints to mature; do not expect a cold email to convert
4. **Chasing volume over activation** — 1,000 signups who never publish a site are worse than 100 who publish and stay; BD success is measured on activated, paying customers
5. **Ignoring Tier 2 cities** — Jeddah, Khobar, Dammam, and Mecca have large SME populations underserved by digital tools; Riyadh is not the only market
